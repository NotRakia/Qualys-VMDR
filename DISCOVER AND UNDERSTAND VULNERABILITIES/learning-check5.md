Which of the following is a good practice for vulnerability scanning?

a. Putting scanners as close to the targets as possible — selected

b. Exclude Severity Level 1 vulnerabilities

c. Selecting all TCP and UDP ports for every scan

d. Exclude Potential vulnerabilities 

Explanation:

By placing scanners as close to their targets as possible, we can limit potential performance issues that can occur when we scan through various network devices, such as routers, firewalls, or load balancers.
