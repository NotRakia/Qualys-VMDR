## Which Qualys sensor is used to scan hosts which have a public-facing IP addresses?

a. Scanner Appliance

  Correctly unselected

b. Out of Band Sensor

  Correctly unselected

c. Virtual Scanner

  Correctly unselected

d. External Scanner // Correctly selected

**Correct**

Scanning your assets from the outside, using the Qualys External Scanners already associated with your account, 
will give you an “external attacker view” of your vulnerabilities from the outside. 
In other words, if you have a Scanner role or above in Qualys, you’ll automatically be able to launch scans against your external (public IP) assets.
