
**Which of the following are elements of the Qualys Detection Score (QDS)? (Select two.)**

a. **Exploit Code Maturity** correct

b. **Trending Risk** correct

c. **Information Gathered QIDs**

d. **Asset Tag**

**Correct**

TruRisk is the brand, but the scoring is made up of 3 components you will see discussed over the next few slides. This scoring system takes into account just how critical the asset (or services it runs) is to your business, and then a understanding of the real risk of all the vulnerabilities on it.

Qualys TruRisk places detected vulnerabilities within the context of your critical and non-critical host assets to help you remediate and fix the vulnerabilities that really count.

Qualys TruRisk is comprised of two components:

1. Qualys Detection Score (QDS)

2. Asset Criticality Score (ACS)

QDS is a calculated value, while ACS is assigned to assets via Asset Tags.
